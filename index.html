About

AI evaluation backed by business and language skills

I am an AI Evaluation and Data Specialist with 4+ years of part-time and freelance experience in LLM evaluation, RLHF-style preference ranking, data annotation and data quality across text, conversational AI and computer vision projects. I have reviewed 2,000+ AI-generated responses for accuracy, reasoning and instruction following.
